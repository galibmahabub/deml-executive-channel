Access protocol

How the channel works

There is no open sign-up. Every account traces back to a single point of issuance.

01

President issues access

The President creates and manages every executive account from the "Manage Executives" tab — no self-registration, ever.

02

Executive signs in

Credentials are handed to the executive directly. Signing in unlocks messages, meetings, profile, and tasks.

03

Channel stays closed

Nothing is indexed, listed, or discoverable outside the channel. Access ends the moment the President revokes it.

Built for confidentiality

Nothing leaves the room

The channel is designed around one principle: only verified executives ever see what happens inside it.

Invitation-only access

Accounts exist only if the President creates them. There is no public sign-up path.

Direct-only messaging

Conversations are addressed to specific participants — no shared feeds, no broadcast channels.

Capped, hosted meetings

Meetings are limited to ten participants and can only be hosted by the President.

Revocable at any time

The President can remove an executive account instantly, cutting off all access in one action.
